In a book I can tell roughly where I am from the weight of the pages in either hand. On a screen, a margin gives me a smaller kind of orientation. It draws a boundary around the place where the writing happens.

## Inside the margin

A margin works best when it relates to what it encloses. On a wide screen the column holds its shape and lets the spare space collect outside it. On a narrow screen the space goes back to the text.
